## Summary
Ninefold: Sudoku has no account of its own, and your progress is stored on your device. The free Android app shows ads provided by Google AdMob (see "Advertising"). If you choose to turn on **online features** (off by default), the app sends the limited data described under "Online features" to our server.

## What is stored on your device
- Game progress: the puzzle in progress, notes, timer, mistakes and hints used
- Results and statistics: games won and lost, best and average times, daily results, streak, achievements, lessons completed
- Settings: theme, accent colour, haptics, sound, mistake limit, reminder time
- A short log of recent results (puzzle ID, difficulty, time, mistakes, hints, date) used only if you choose to share it

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## What we do not collect
We do not collect your real name, email, phone number or contacts, and we do not run analytics ourselves. Advertising partners may collect the data described under "Advertising".

## Advertising
The Android app shows ads served by **Google AdMob** (Google LLC):
- **Rewarded videos** that you choose to watch to unlock an extra hint
- **Occasional full-screen ads** after a puzzle, not during play

To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interaction data, and may use cookies or similar technologies, as described in Google's policies (https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). We do not receive your personal information from them.
- **Choices:** in regions where consent is required (such as the EEA and UK) the app asks for your choice before ads load, and you can change it later in Settings > Ad privacy choices. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your device's settings.
- Ad-free builds (for example, platforms where ads are not enabled) show no ads and send no advertising data.

## Google Play Games leaderboards (optional)
If you choose to join, the app signs you in with **Google Play Games Services** and posts the time of daily puzzles you solve without hints, and your daily streak, to Google's leaderboards. Google shows these to other players according to your Play Games profile and privacy settings, and processes them under Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can leave the leaderboards or change your Play Games profile visibility in the Google Play Games app, and the game works the same if you never join.

## Checking your connection
The Android app is supported by ads, which need the internet. To know whether you are online it periodically requests a very small file from Google's connectivity-check server (connectivitycheck.gstatic.com). This reveals your IP address to Google, like any web request, and nothing else.

## Optional features
- **Daily reminder:** if you turn it on, the app asks for notification permission and schedules a notification on your device at the time you pick. No server is involved. You can turn it off in Settings or in your system settings.
- **Share result:** tapping Share opens your device's share sheet with a picture and text of your result. What happens next depends on the app you choose.
- **Share solve data:** in Settings you can send a file with puzzle IDs, times, mistakes and hints to help us tune difficulty. Nothing is sent unless you tap it and choose where to send it.

## Online features on our own server (optional, off by default, only in builds that include it)
Only if you switch on **Online features** in Settings (and only in builds where a server is configured), the app creates an anonymous account and sends:
- **Daily results:** the date, puzzle ID, your time, number of mistakes and hints, and the list of digits you entered with their timing (used to check the result is genuine)
- **Display name:** a random name until you change it; it is shown to other players on leaderboards and to your friends
- **Friends:** the invite codes you create or accept, and who your friends are
- **Backup (optional):** a compact summary of your streak, statistics, lessons, achievements and completed daily dates, so you can restore your progress on a new phone using a recovery code you create
- **Puzzle updates:** the app downloads new puzzle packs from our server (this reveals your IP address to the server, as any download does)

We keep this data to run these features. You can delete your online account at any time in Settings > Online features > Friends and account > Delete online account; this removes your display name, results, friends and backup from our server. Your progress on your device is unaffected. We do not sell this data or use it for advertising.
